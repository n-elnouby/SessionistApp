Privacy Policy

Sessionist — Game Time Tracker for iPhone, iPad and Mac • Effective November 16, 2026

Overview

Sessionist has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your library stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. The developer only receives data you choose to share through the optional Community Lengths feature described below.

Data Storage

Everything you enter in Sessionist — your game library, sessions, notes, ratings, collections and wishlist — is stored on your device. If you use iCloud, it is synced privately through your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. The developer has no access to this data.

On the Mac, Sessionist reads files on your computer to find and track games: installed apps, the Steam, Epic and GOG library folders you choose, and the GeForce NOW log if you allow it. These files are only read on your Mac and are never sent anywhere.

Community Lengths (optional)

If you turn on “Share Playtime of Finished Games” in Settings, Sessionist sends the playtime of games you mark as finished to Sessionist's public iCloud database. For each game it sends: the game's IGDB id, your total playtime, how much of it Sessionist's timer measured and how much came from Steam, the number of sessions, the length you compared against (main story, main + extras or completionist), the platform, and whether it came from the iPhone or Mac app and which version.

It does not send your name, email address, Apple ID or game titles. Apple's iCloud stores each report together with an identifier that is specific to Sessionist, so you have one report per game and can delete your reports at any time: turn the setting off and choose “Stop and Remove What I Shared”. Other players only see combined figures for a game (the median and the range most players took), and only once at least three players have shared it. Sharing is off until you turn it on.

Game Information and Prices

To show game details, covers, lengths, trailers, events and prices, Sessionist looks games up through a small server run by the developer on Cloudflare, which forwards the request to IGDB (owned by Twitch), Steam or the App Store. These requests contain what is needed to answer them, such as the search text, game ids or your store region. They contain no personal information, and the server does not store them; it only caches public game and price information for a short time. As with any web request, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver it.

Steam Sync (optional)

If you link your Steam profile, Sessionist sends your Steam profile id through the same server to Valve's Steam Web API to read your games, playtime and achievements. Your Steam profile id is kept on your device; the server passes it on and does not store it. Steam only returns this data for profiles set to public. You can unlink Steam in Settings at any time.

Links and Videos

Sessionist links to stores, HowLongToBeat, YouTube and similar sites, and shows trailer thumbnails from YouTube. When you open a link or a thumbnail loads, that site receives the request under its own privacy policy.

Analytics and Tracking

Sessionist does not include any analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks or tracking tools, and does not track you across apps or websites. If you have chosen to share crash reports with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to the developer.

In-App Purchases

Sessionist offers optional tip jar purchases to support development. These transactions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store any payment information.

Services Used

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit — syncing your library, and Community Lengths if you turn it on
  • Cloudflare — the developer's server for game, price and Steam lookups
  • IGDB (Twitch) — game information and events
  • Valve Steam Web API — Steam Sync and Steam prices
  • Apple App Store — App Store prices and tip jar purchases

Children's Privacy

Sessionist does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or any personal information to function.
